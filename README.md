# DM Save Card

Turn your **Delta Manager** save into Discord-ready cards and stories: race results, season summaries, playthrough chapters, teammate duels and more. Multiplayer works too: paste the race summary, no save needed.

**▶ Open the tool: https://ctrlderk.github.io/dm-savecard-generator/**

- Works in your browser. Best in Chrome or Edge (remembers your save folder and updates after every save).
- Your save is read on your own PC. Nothing is uploaded; seasons and stories stay in your browser (use **Backup**).
- Posting: **Copy for Discord**, or a webhook from your server's moderators. Nobody is pinged.
- Stuck? Click **? Help** in the tool.

Feedback and bugs: open an issue here.

DM Save Card is a community tool, not part of Delta Manager.
